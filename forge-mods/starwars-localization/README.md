5. **Topmost & Clobbered Support:** Serves curated v2.3 overrides even when the Star Wars pack's `TEXT` archive is already topmost (`SW_LOC_CURATED_LOADED`), and stays completely inactive (`0` hooks) in Regular/Vanilla Helldivers 2.
