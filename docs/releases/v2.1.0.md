# Clonedivers 2.1.0

> **Release candidate (`v2.1.0`):** Paired with [Linux Forge `0.10.0`](linux-v0.10.0.md), **EmpireDivers `2026.11.22-r49`**, **Clonedivers `2026.11.22-r21`**, and gameplay catalog **`build25480438-r8`** (`17` verified archives, `0` `hf-mod.com` URLs).

## Highlights

- **5 Native Star Wars Texture Tiers (Up to `-80%` VRAM & `-66%` Disk):** Both **EmpireDivers (`r49`)** and **Clonedivers (`r21`)** now ship 5 real pack tiers end-to-end — **Full (Ultra)**, **High (Streamed)**, **1024px (Medium)**, **512px (Low / Deck)**, and **256px + Decimated Meshes (Ultra-Potato)** (`1.73 GB` active GPU / `3.88 GB` disk on Empire; `1.93 GB` active GPU / `2.98 GB` disk on Clones).
- **Empire `r49` & Clones `r21` Visual & Rig Overhaul:**
  - **Solid Folded-Wing HD Lambda Shuttle:** Replaces the broken extraction Pelican with a full-detail Imperial Lambda shuttle whose wings fold cleanly on landing without transparent-panel artifacts.
  - **AT-ST Across All 4 Exosuits (`empire-atst-writer 0.2.2`):** Full film-scale AT-ST support across `EXO-45`, `EXO-49`, `EXO-51`, and `EXO-55` with ABA-safe slot tracking.
  - **500+ Armor & Rig Repairs:** Fixes `516` Empire and `31` Clones `UNIT`/mesh/skeleton bindings across all 5 tiers.
  - **Encrypted Death Trooper Voice:** Imperial voice slot 4 now defaults to Rogue One scrambled Death Trooper comms (toggleable under **Customize → Advanced**).
- **3-Way Covenant + Squids Mix:** New installs default to **Mixed Covenant + Squids** alongside **Mixed Bots + CIS** (`Full Star Wars Mix`), with a 3-way **Mixed Covenant / All Covenant / Native Squids** selector in **Customize → Advanced**.
- **Gameplay & Co-op Code Mods (`build25480438-r8`):**
  - **O(1) Camera Pullback (`0.1.2`):** Automatic third-person camera clearance for AT-ST / AT-TE walkers and Compact LEGO kits at `< 0.5 µs/frame` on Difficulty 11 (`65k` units), with Havok wall clamping and instant ADS/FPV bypass.
  - **Difficulty 11 Mega City Chaos OOM Fix (`chaos-invasions 0.3.3`):** Defers foreign faction preloads out of `PrepareMission` into paced `HF_STATE_MISSION` loading with a 120s slow-joiner barrier.
  - **Dedicated Melee Slot (`1.5.2-clonedivers.2`):** Carry a pistol and a melee weapon together in 4-player co-op with a non-colliding `patch_920` loadout picker.
  - **Host-Gated Faster Corpse Cleanup (`1.0.6-clonedivers.1`) & Heavy SEAF (`1.2.0-clonedivers.5`):** Co-op joiner-safe corpse cleanup, 10-arg `ActivateStratagemBeacon` ABI fix, 15s ground landing gate, and 1-unit/tick squad equip pacing.
- **Clear Launch Staging & Overhaul Collision Guard:** Shows **`Launching Helldivers 2 — please wait…`** with a live pulsing bar and **`Cancel Launch`** button during the 15–30s pre-window stage, and automatically disables stray/demo Star Wars overhaul packs so two overhauls never collide.
