# Clonedivers 1.6.1

Launcher **1.6.1** with pack **2026.09.24-r12**. The launcher itself is unchanged apart from
its version; the update is the pack. Accept the launcher update, then choose **Update pack**
(about 155 MB from r10, or 91 MB if you already took r11).

- **New opening:** a Republic Venator fleet dropping out of hyperspace (animation by
  [Coastlake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkVDSAyDkhE)), ending on its own fade to black.
  It replaces the Republic Commando opening.
- **Boss sounds like Temuera Morrison throughout.** He keeps his Republic Commando lines;
  263 calls that previously played Battlefront clone lines or silence now use Temuera Morrison
  clone lines. Sev, Fixer and Scorch are unchanged.
- **Spider Droid aim markers:** red markers show the War Strider's eye (a bullseye on the
  front of the ball) and back vent (a reticle behind the neck). They require **Droid skins**.
  The top eye is not marked; it sits inside the Spider Droid body.

Not yet checked in game: the new opening, Boss's added lines and the markers. If the game
closes during the opening, choose **Helldivers** to play and report what happened. Supply FRV
and Watcher audio remain experimental. HD2 build 25480438 (7.1.1).
