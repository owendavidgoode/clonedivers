# EmpireDivers ships, voices and presentation

Pack **2026.09.27-r19** for launcher **1.7.1**. Keep the game closed, choose
**EmpireDivers**, then **Update pack**. The launcher executable is unchanged.

- Three detailed Star Destroyer models replace the lower-detail donor. Hull maps
  retain their **6144 × 6144** source resolution. The conversion preserves the
  current game's rig, physics and event metadata and updates old vertex formats.
  Stock texture dependencies resolve from the current game.
- Both cape models have every draw group and index count cleared, including
  shadow geometry. This later Empire-only override reinforces the shared cape mod.
- **784 player-callout assignments** across four voice slots use curated short
  stormtrooper recordings: 199/193/196/196 of each slot's 785 native sound sources.
  Reload, reinforcement, grenade, injury, enemy and squad calls use explicit
  category mappings rather than the donor's unrelated ambient chatter.
- **766 distinct Imperial command/pilot streams** retain current native routing.
  Shared media accounts for overlapping bank totals. Admiral, mission control,
  Eagle and extraction/shuttle dialogue come from the Imperial donor; four
  compatible tutorial streams are also retained.
- Imperial galactic-map icons, map labels and PA audio. Donor text is overlaid on
  current English tables so new game strings remain available.
- SEAF weapon and foley effects are separated from Clonedivers' mixed voice/effect
  bundle. Clone SEAF voices and exertions are excluded.

Clonedivers is unchanged, including its Venator intro and Clone/Commando voices.
Empire keeps the full shared scope/weapon setup, SAI E-11 sound and blue bolts,
gray LAAT, AT-STs and battle intro. Covenant remains independent.

This is a **partial curated voice conversion**, not new voice acting or complete
dialogue coverage. Unmapped player lines and exertions retain HD2 audio. Command
recordings follow the donor's event assignments; their wording is not guaranteed
to reproduce HD2's precise tactical information. The Imperial ship textures retain
their full resolution in both texture profiles. Gameplay and runtime mixing have
not been tested.

Validation: 32 launcher mode selections across both profiles and enemy toggles;
Clonedivers byte-for-byte isolation; preserved shared weapons/SAI; current audio
media/codec checks; current text-key preservation; three independently decoded
ship models with finite geometry and valid indices; two zero-triangle cape models.
All 338 distinct donor stormtrooper clips received local automated transcription;
selected short callouts were reviewed against those transcripts. This is not a
human listening assessment. Command audio received sampled transcription checks.
