# Pack 2026.10.08-r21 / launcher 1.7.3

This cumulative Empire update promotes the current reviewed private pack to the
public combined Clonedivers, CommandoDivers and EmpireDivers release. It retains
Full and Lighter profiles, all existing options, equipment mappings and the
published launcher 1.7.3 camera defaults. No launcher downgrade is included.

The pack includes 18 Imperial armor styles across 21 named sets and 23 retained
compatibility routes, including the literal LEGO bikini Stormtrooper. Armor names,
black underlayers, both body selectors and retained attachment routes are included.
The earlier unwanted human bikini model is excluded.

Vehicle work includes the 9.04-meter baked AT-ST with scaled native rigs and
boarding routes, Lambda shuttle geometry and articulation, Star Destroyer main
and background hull updates, and TIE Fighter/Bomber models and sounds. The guarded
Eagle helper retains distinct strafing and bombing routes and requires Full
textures for its reviewed runtime closure. Its installation
checks now resolve each user's own launcher settings and installation, rather
than the developer's private paths/feed. The walker capture diagnostic is disabled
by default in the public pack; the baked walker does not depend on that capture.

Audio and weapon work includes retained E-Web/FWMB source fits, HMG/Maxigun audio,
red effects, the 217 previously authored voice assignments and 303 authentic
nonverbal replacements. The final deep QA pass corrects the Clone/Commando cutscene
PCM declaration, restores exact native streamed prefetch metadata, normalizes
Commando hip weights, and repairs Lambda personnel and two AT-ST visibility bounds.

The promotion retains all 1,794 logical rows in their original order with their
mode, texture and option conditions. Game payloads match the sealed deep-QA source
except the two explicitly reviewed portable helper contexts. Existing immutable
hosted asset URLs are reused; new assets use SHA256 names.

## Validation and known limits

The private checkpoint passed its whole-pack offline audit and all 96 requested
launcher states. The public promotion additionally checks the actual published
1.7.3 binary, Full/Lighter source hashes and portable-helper positive/rejection
fixtures before publication. These checks establish packaging and guard behavior,
not game acceptance.

Star Destroyer rendering remains unconfirmed after the owner's missing-hull report.
The 120 model-specific armor damage/cap routes remain unfinished. Film-scale walker
physics, boarding and animation, mission drop/extraction, Eagle dispatch, and actual
voice/audio mix still require gameplay testing. The separate missing native
Constitution sound source remains unresolved; no guessed replacement was added.

First Person remains experimental. On each new session, while first person is
disabled aboard the ship, set Engine camera bridge On > Apply > Off > Apply.
Leave EXPERIMENT sync camera to game off, and enable first person only after landing.
Launcher 1.7.3 starts fresh installations with first person disabled and preserves
existing saved choices.
