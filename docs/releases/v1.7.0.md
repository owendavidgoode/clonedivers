# Clonedivers 1.7.0

Launcher **1.7.0**, current pack **2026.09.26-r16** (original release: r15). Update the launcher first, then choose
your universe with Helldivers 2 closed. Your existing Clonedivers setup stays
selected; unchanged downloads are reused.

- **LAS-12 SAI:** E-11 firing sound and blue Battlefront-style blaster bolts in
  Clonedivers and EmpireDivers. Gun model unchanged. Added in the small
  [r16 pack update](pack-2026.09.26-r16.md); gameplay unverified.
- **EmpireDivers:** a third universe with Stormtrooper armor, Star Destroyers,
  a gray LAAT, shared blasters, and AT-ST Patriot/Emancipator exosuits. Use
  **B-01 Tactical with Brawny** or **Bloodhound with Lean** for Imperial armor.
  Other armor, player voices, opening, and Lumberer/Bastion remain vanilla.
- **Covenant squids:** optional models and voices for core Illuminate units,
  available in either modded universe. Newer units remain vanilla. Watcher probe
  audio is disabled while Covenant is enabled to avoid conflicts.
- Mode switching preserves Covenant, Droid skins and JohnsonPotatoMode choices.
  Clonedivers with Covenant off retains the r14 content plus r16's SAI bundle. Helldivers parks
  all mods as before. No new music is included.

**Experimental content:** the owner authorized this release without an in-game
test. The old AT-ST was repaired for current asset formats, but startup, walking,
aiming, weapons, entry/exit and destruction have not been verified. Armor and
Covenant animation, sounds and hitboxes also need gameplay checking. The
Stormtrooper source has known shoulder deformation; Covenant Elite headshot
zones may align around the jaw rather than the visible head.

Offline validation passed: native installer tests and mode round trips, 16
option/profile checks, geometry decoding (51 Imperial, 13 Covenant, 2 AT-ST body
models), and structural checks for four hidden AT-ST weapons. Exporter-only
workarounds for empty meshes are recorded; this is not gameplay evidence.
