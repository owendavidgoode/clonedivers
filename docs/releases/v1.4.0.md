# Clonedivers 1.4.0

Choose your universe: **Helldivers**, **Clonedivers**, or **Commandodivers**.
Each mode has its own helmet button. Delta Squad is elite.

Download **Clonedivers.exe**, replace your old copy while it is closed, then open
it and choose **Update pack**. Existing 1.3 users can also use the launcher's app
update offer. The matching pack is **2026.09.12-r7**; unchanged files are reused.
Keep Helldivers 2 closed while updating or switching modes.

Lighter textures and Hide weapon sights keep their saved settings. Helldivers
mode parks the mods; both modded modes use the verified, recoverable installer.

For character selection and equipment requirements, see
[team setup](https://github.com/owendavidgoode/clonedivers/blob/main/docs/TEAM-SETUP.md).
Camera distance is unchanged. FOV remains a personal game setting.
