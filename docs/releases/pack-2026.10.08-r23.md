# Pack 2026.10.08-r23

EmpireDivers adds the classic LEGO Star Wars Yoda death scream to the local player's LEGO Stormtrooper (DP-40) and LEGO Bikini Stormtrooper (AF-02) body armor. Helmets do not control the effect. The trigger requires an observed native dead state, matching applied armor and avatar identity, and a loaded sound event; it posts once per death. Ordinary armor, teammate deaths, avatar removal, respawns and ship transitions are excluded.

The sound is a separate event added to the common player voice bank. Existing sound objects and recordings are preserved. The original Complete Saga recording is 1.334 seconds; its decoded PCM is unchanged. Launcher 1.7.3 and the previous pack contents remain included. The existing Eagle controller receives only the new pack-version binding.

Offline qualification and installed-file verification do not establish in-game acceptance. Test one death in each LEGO armor, a death in ordinary armor, and a respawn/mission exit. The broader r22 playtest checklist remains applicable.

Recording: TT Games / LucasArts, LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga, `Character Voices/YODA/YODADEATH.WAV`.
