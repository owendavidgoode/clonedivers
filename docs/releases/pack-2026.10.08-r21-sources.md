## Format and conversion research

- [JeremyAnsel.Xwa.Opt](https://github.com/JeremyAnsel/JeremyAnsel.Xwa.Opt), version 3.0.56, and [JeremyAnsel's XWA hooks](https://github.com/JeremyAnsel/xwa_hooks/tree/fada698c9d0a1ff5e0b6d7317126c77756561bfc) supplied OPT decoding and S-foil reference evidence. These are tool/research credits, not attribution of the original game models to the tool author.
- [UEViewer / UModel](https://github.com/gildor2/UEViewer/tree/a0bfb468d42be831b126632fd8a0ae6b3614f981) supplied the pinned Fallen Order model export and primary Unreal package-format references.
- [GlyphX Tools format documentation](https://glyphxtools.github.io/file-formats/alo-model-format.html) supplied ALO, MEG and chunk-format references for the Empire at War reader.
- [linterniGamer's TT Games extraction references](https://github.com/linterniGamer/Tt-Games-quickbms-scripts/blob/master/files/ttgames_TSS.bms) supplied source format research for the Skywalker Saga DAT reader.
- [SkyeShade / HD2Runtime](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime/tree/96ab2d258d867a5df4f22bb7b3321d84d28de21d) and [HD2SDK Community Edition](https://github.com/Boxofbiscuits97/HD2SDK-CommunityEdition/tree/0b283cb52669f2600d51277f51dbb0a07c00cc52) supplied pinned runtime, archive and native resource-format research.

These references describe provenance and technical contribution. They do not
certify gameplay acceptance or grant rights to unrelated source assets.
