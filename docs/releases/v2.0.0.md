## Turnkey Setup Wizard & Launcher UX (`F1`–`F16`)

- **4-Step Single Setup Wizard (`HellForgeSetupForm`):** Consolidates fresh onboarding and repair into one modal wizard with step pills:
  1. **Step 1 · Preflight Check:** Verifies Steam sign-in, `70 GB` (`70 GiB`) free space on the target Forge drive, and destination folder outside Steam and OneDrive.
  2. **Step 2 · Forge & Game Copy:** Installs/updates verified `HellForge 0.4.0-beta` (`engine 0.1.47`, `Mod SDK 16`), copies build `25480438` read-only from Steam when matching or downloads via in-app Steam Mobile App QR approval (plus **Official setup (fallback)**), and enables sandbox/WFP isolation.
  3. **Step 3 · Guided Steam Account Sync (Required):** Enforces mandatory one-way HD2 -> Forge account sync (no skip or fresh-character bypass), detects active `hosts` capture-proxy state (`OPEN HELLFORGE TO CLEAN UP`), and auto-imports the sealed capture once HD2 and HellForge close.
  4. **Step 4 · Pack Download & Ready:** Stages and verifies the 14-archive `build25480438-r5` gameplay preset, exports/imports the selected appearance pack with resumable HTTP Range byte progress, and returns to **Ready to launch** with a first-launch Windows Firewall approval notice (`HasRun == false`).
- **Unified Appearance Controls (`Clones` & `Empire`):**
  - **Enemy skin (`3` options):** `All CIS Droids`, `Mixed Bots + CIS Droids` (`32` Battlefront CIS `UNIT`s alongside `45` native Automaton Hulks, Devastators, Berserkers, Factory Striders, and 7.0+ units), or `Native Automatons`.
  - **Textures (`3` tiers):** `Full`, `Lighter (Streamed)`, or `Johnson Ultra-Potato (512px)` (`reduced512`: `512 px` max cap / `128 px` resident tail for low-VRAM GPUs) with contextual VRAM advisor guidance.
  - **Optional faction toggles:** `Delta Squad` armor/voices in Clones, and opt-in `Covenant` (`1670`) Illuminate conversion in both Clones and Empire.
- **Single-instance focus & fast-crash surfacing:** Launching a second `Clonedivers.exe` activates and restores the running window instead of spawning a duplicate (`F15`), and failed sessions (`lastStatus: failed` / `cleanup_failed`) surface **Last session needs attention** immediately (`F6`).
