# Clonedivers 1.6.0

Clones and commandos now share one roster. Choose **Helldivers** for the original
game or **Clonedivers** for the squad pack, then update the pack to **2026.09.24-r10**.

- Fixed missing/blurry ADS scopes; the replacement blasters keep clear aiming views.
- Added more clone armor colors and clearer Commando equipment names. Commando
  helmets are limited to four targets. Commando bodies still require **Brawny**.
- Delta Squad voices and the opening are always included in Clonedivers. Voice
  choices remain Sev, Fixer, Scorch and Boss; armor does not select a voice automatically.
- Expanded RC dialogue and refreshed English text/audio banks for HD2 7.1; verified unchanged in 7.1.1.
- Added **Droid skins** and **JohnsonPotatoMode™** switches. Potato mode streams
  full-detail textures; it is not a reduced-resolution pack or a promised FPS gain.
- Restored MTT cannons and spider-droid weapons. Eye/vent markers remain unfinished.
- Added the Falchion Bastion replacement and an **experimental** TX-130 Supply FRV port.
- Removed Guard Dog probe chatter and moved it to an **experimental** Watcher loop.
- Added local session reports and optional automatic performance sharing with Owen.
  Sharing needs a squad invitation and one-time setup under Help & diagnostics.
- Regular Helldivers hides all mod options. Cached mode changes stay quiet.

ADS passed on the 7.1 candidate; startup passed on 7.1.1 (build 25480438). Supply FRV seating, supplies,
deformation and destruction, Watcher loop timing/stopping, and walker weapon
alignment still need squad gameplay checks. Those experimental additions are included
at the owner's request. Some voice calls retain fallback lines; Boss gain is unchanged.

The AT-TE camera is unchanged. This release contains neither the rejected cutaway
nor the experimental Lua camera loader/probe. A Steam “Running” status after game
exit has also been reproduced with manually started Steam; it remains unresolved.

Existing players: update the launcher first, then choose **Update pack**. Downloads
reuse unchanged files. Older launchers keep their prior pack until the app update.
