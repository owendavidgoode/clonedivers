**How to update:** if you run 1.3.0 or 1.3.1, the app offers this update itself: click the "Clonedivers 1.3.2 is available" line under the pack buttons. Otherwise download **Clonedivers.exe** below and replace your old one.

**Fixed in 1.3.2**
- Switching a toggle such as **Lighter textures** off, on, and off again no longer re-downloads the files it parked the first time. `mods_old\` never overwrites, so a file parked a second time under a taken name gets a `.1` suffix; the app now recognises those copies and renames them back into place instead of fetching them again.

Everything else is 1.3.1: the **Lighter textures** toggle for 16 GB PCs, small incremental pack updates, the self-updater, and the game-update warning.
