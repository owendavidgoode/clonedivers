# Clonedivers pack 2026.09.24-r11

Launcher stays **1.6.0**. Close the game, open Clonedivers and choose **Update pack**
(about 94 MB; unchanged files are reused).

- **New opening:** a Republic Venator fleet dropping out of hyperspace (animation by
  [Coastlake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkVDSAyDkhE)). It replaces the Republic
  Commando opening.
- **Boss sounds like Temuera Morrison throughout.** He keeps his Republic Commando lines.
  263 calls that previously played Battlefront clone lines or silence now use Temuera
  Morrison clone lines. Sev, Fixer and Scorch are unchanged.
- **Spider Droid aim markers:** red markers show the War Strider's eye (a bullseye on the
  front of the ball) and back vent (a reticle behind the neck). They require **Droid skins**.
  The top eye is not marked; it sits inside the Spider Droid body.

Not yet checked in game: the new opening, Boss's added lines and the markers. If the
game closes during the opening, choose **Helldivers** to play and report what happened.
