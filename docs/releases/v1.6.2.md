# Clonedivers 1.6.2

Launcher **1.6.2** with pack **2026.09.24-r13**. The launcher itself is unchanged apart from
its version. Accept the launcher update, then choose **Update pack** (about 46 MB from r12).

- **Fewer silent voice lines:** 318 lines that were silent or used a Battlefront clone voice
  now use Republic Commando recordings from that character, or Temuera Morrison clone lines:
  - enemy and walker pings ("Enemy spotted.", "Spider Droid!");
  - "strategy selected" ("Ready, sir.");
  - sample and resource pickups ("Got it.");
  - finds and item pings ("There's something here.");
  - wildlife, and "I win" / "You win" / "Let's play".
  Lines that already had the right voice are unchanged.
- **Voice 4 is now "Boss/Clone":** Boss's Republic Commando lines with Temuera Morrison clone
  lines filling his other calls.
- Still silent: numbers, NATO letters, compass directions and distances. No clone recordings
  of these exist in the available sources.

Also included from 1.6.1: the Venator fleet opening, Boss's Temuera lines and the Spider
Droid eye/vent markers (with **Droid skins**). None of the voice, opening or marker changes has
been played in game yet. If the game closes during the opening, choose **Helldivers** to play
and report what happened. HD2 build 25480438 (7.1.1).
