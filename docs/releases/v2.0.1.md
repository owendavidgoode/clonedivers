## Difficulty 11 / High-Unit-Count Performance Hotfix (`build25480438-r6`)

- **Root cause fixed:** During high-unit-count Difficulty 11 combat (`UnitManager` capacity up to `262,144` slots across 16 render worker threads), `clonedivers.lego-compact-leaf-writer 0.1.0` acquired an exclusive `SRWLOCK` on every `512B80` skinned-mesh call, ran full `UnitManager` copies (up to `2.35 MB`) and scans on negative-cache misses under that global lock, cleared its negative cache every `500 ms`, and wrote `85` diagnostic `.bin` dumps until hitting `log_budget` (`fatal reason=3`). `clonedivers.empire-atst-writer 0.1.0` also acquired its exclusive `SRWLOCK` on every `512B80`/`1E7CD0` call even with zero walkers active.
- **`clonedivers.lego-compact-leaf-writer` `0.2.0`** (`240,032` B, SHA-256 `e26ccf4100d01e51fd4afa2ae2dcd6264393b9aafaa2e1a0a37b3cc98dcf3393`, ContentHash `907f07bc93478bc56a5ad881382246af9d3907534e2fa5065a0e99e882e09c8b`):
  - Adds a 64-byte-aligned lock-free seqlock `CoopFastFilter` (`__atomic_load_n` acquire loads, zero shared atomic writes on reject) in `512B80` and `1E7CD0`.
  - Replaces hot-path `InterlockedCompareExchange`/`InterlockedCompareExchangePointer` reads in `writer_core.c` with pure acquire loads.
  - Removes all `UnitManager` scanning from `512B80`; moves discovery to 250 ms `WriterTick` with O(active) point reads and bounded `16,384`-slot incremental slices.
  - Checks O(4) `coopReg.players[]` avatar handles first in `coop_find_slot_ptr`.
  - Gates per-invocation `.bin` diagnostic capture behind an `.arm` opt-in file.
- **`clonedivers.empire-atst-writer` `0.2.0`** (`35,362` B, SHA-256 `6095726add3aafd351cc8c6269c42ebdb4ba9c691c56a9772bc1d5db63a9857c`, ContentHash `c908db59cdf6e3d6091b7403820bb22f6e14e4222be7244fc3527a4ee8d3f6aa`):
  - Adds a 64-byte-aligned lock-free seqlock `at_fast_filter` (separated from `SRWLOCK lock`) with pure acquire loads and zero shared atomic writes on reject — zero locks and zero memory reads when no walkers are active.
  - Removes `at_registry_reconcile` from `512B80`; smooth `2,048`-slot per-frame rolling scan with a 56-byte vtable/resource pre-check and per-slot fallback on bulk chunk read failure.
- **Automatic `r5 -> r6` upgrade:** Existing `2.0.0` installations automatically upgrade enabled `0.1.0` LEGO and AT-ST leaf writers to `0.2.0` while preserving disabled/removed optional choices.
