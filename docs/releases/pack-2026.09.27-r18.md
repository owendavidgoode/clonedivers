# EmpireDivers shared equipment and effects

Pack **2026.09.27-r18** for launcher **1.7.1**. Keep the game closed and select
**Update pack**. No new launcher executable is required.

Empire's original shared-content list omitted the later scope/ADS build and many
equipment, sound and vehicle improvements. This update enables **126 existing
bundles** in Empire with their already-shipped Clonedivers content intact:

- All **90 scope/ADS archives**, including the global lens, reticle and material
  dependencies. Existing blaster models, effects, attachment cleanup and SAI E-11
  sound/blue bolts remain included.
- Backpacks, shields, jump/hover packs, GNK hellbomb and invisible capes.
- Stratagem input sounds, Eagle effects, orbital and sentry sounds, autocannon,
  shuttle and walker audio, and the same enemy audio setup as Clonedivers.
- Y-Wing Eagle, LAAT/c transport and transport cleanup, TX-130 FRV, the Supply FRV
  port and Falchion Bastion, with matching Full/Lighter assets.

Empire retains its Imperial armor, Star Destroyers, gray LAAT, AT-ST
Patriot/Emancipator and battle intro. Clone/Commando player, officer, SEAF and
pilot voices stay exclusive to Clonedivers, as requested. Clone armor, Republic
map presentation and AT-TE-specific models/animations also remain exclusive.
Clonedivers, including its Venator intro, is unchanged. Covenant remains optional
in both modes and keeps its Watcher-audio conflict handling.

The additional Empire selection is about **1.28 GB Full / 1.31 GB Lighter** before
cache reuse. Players who already downloaded Clonedivers can reuse matching files;
the launcher computes the actual missing download. This release adds no new asset
bytes to GitHub and does not alter any asset URL or digest.

Validation covers 32 mode selections across profiles, enemy toggles and stale
Delta preferences, exact unchanged Clonedivers output, complete weapon/scope
membership, source hashes and gap-free patch numbering. The resource audit checks
540 shared resource identities against the 148 Imperial identities. Only the
intentional intro SFX bank/dependency overlap remains; the Empire intro wins.
The promoted audio banks are effects, enemy, weapon and vehicle banks, not clone
voice banks. Gameplay remains unverified; existing Supply FRV and vehicle risks
still apply.

Rebuild: `tools/build-empire-parity.py`. Validate with `tools/EmpireParity.Check`.
