# SEAF compatibility distribution

On November 5, 2026 (UTC), the project owner confirmed in this development chat
that they have written permission from both NontoxicKappa (Heavy SEAF) and
JohnnyGrease (SEAF Squad Stratagems) to distribute the Clonedivers compatibility
fixes. This records the owner's confirmation; the underlying correspondence is
not published here.

That confirmation covers the tested Heavy `1.2.0-clonedivers.3` and Squad
`0.1.4-clonedivers.2` packages. Retain their original credits, sources and notices.
It does not relicense the originals or authorize mirroring unrelated mods.
The compatible archives remain byte-identical to the owner-tested packages.

Other dependencies are obtained directly from their authors. In particular,
Guard Dogs and Smarter SEAF are not rehosted. Automatic host mod sharing remains
off because these author packages have their own redistribution restrictions.

Pinned compatibility/gameplay downloads and content hashes are in
`Clonedivers/Resources/forge-gameplay-build25480438-r1.json`. The loader, menu,
Smarter SEAF, Guard Dogs and Reroller use immutable author file/release URLs.
No silent upgrade to a newer Reroller or another engine is part of this preset.
